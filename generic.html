PROJECT 01

Serenity AI

Your personal space for a calmer mind and a healthier digital lifestyle. Serenity AI is an AI-powered mental wellness platform that offers mood-based music, breathing exercises, and mindfulness recommendations to help users manage everyday stress and anxiety.

About the Project

In today's busy world, stress and overthinking can affect our daily lives and emotional well-being. Serenity AI aims to make mental wellness simple, accessible, and personal through a calm and user-friendly digital experience.

The platform allows users to select their current mood and receive suitable wellness suggestions. Based on their emotional state, users can explore relaxing music, guided breathing exercises, and mindfulness activities that encourage them to pause, relax, and reconnect with themselves.

The goal of Serenity AI is to create a peaceful digital space where technology supports emotional well-being through personalized and easy-to-follow recommendations.

Key Features

  • Mood-Based Recommendations: Personalized suggestions based on the user's selected mood.
  • Breathing Exercises: Simple breathing activities to encourage relaxation.
  • Relaxing Music: Music suggestions designed to create a peaceful listening experience.
  • Mindfulness Support: Wellness tips that encourage calmness and self-awareness.
  • Simple User Interface: A clean and calming design focused on ease of use.

Technologies Used

Programming Language: Java
Backend: Spring Boot, REST APIs
Database: MySQL
Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Build Tool: Maven

What I Learned

Working on Serenity AI helped me strengthen my Java programming and backend development skills. I gained practical experience with Spring Boot, REST APIs, database integration using MySQL, and organizing an application into structured components.

This project also helped me understand how thoughtful design and technology can work together to create a meaningful user experience.
